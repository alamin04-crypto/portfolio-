A professional A4 CV built from the data currently in this editor (Name, Bio, Contact, Status and each sector's Experience, Education, Skills, Languages and Samples). Each sector gets its own separate CV, and the combined CV puts every sector on the site into one file. Your profile photo is included full-frame at 100% — never cropped: if no custom photo is saved yet, upload one in the Hero section above first, otherwise the CV shows your initials instead. Each button opens a print-ready CV in a new tab — there choose Print → Save as PDF (it also tries to open the print dialog by itself).
